Fortune Cookies Ultimate — Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app Fortune Cookies Ultimate (package com.fortunecookie) handles. The app is made by an independent developer, who can be reached at slothatvork@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, sign-ins or registration.
  • The developer does not run any server and does not collect, receive or sell your personal information.
  • Your fortunes, History, Collection, statistics and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The optional Epic Cookie shows an ad from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show that ad, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the fortunes you open, the fortunes you keep, your opening statistics and your settings (motion, text size and sound) in the app's private storage on your phone. This information never leaves your device through the app. You can erase it at any time in Settings → Data, or by uninstalling the app.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by the developer.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Epic Cookie offers a rewarded ad: you choose to watch it to open that cookie. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location,
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system and language), and
  • information about ad interactions and app performance, used for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention.

This information is collected and used by Google, not by the developer. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings.

Sharing a fortune

When you tap Share, the app creates a picture of the selected fortune and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you pick an app and send it yourself; the receiving app's own privacy policy then applies.

Permissions

The app uses internet and network-state access only so that ads can load. It does not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Children

The app is intended for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact the developer at the email address above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
